Why Volunteer?

  • Strengthen Community Connections.

    Contribute to a shared event that brings families together and promotes local engagement. Meet like-minded people who care about youth and healthy living.

  • Inspire and support young athletes.

    Help kids build confidence as they achieve their goals. Be a positive role model by encouraging perseverance and sportsmanship.

  • Promote healthy lifestyles.

    Encourage kids to develop habits of physical activity and wellness. Set an example of giving back and staying active in the community.

  • Give back to the community.

    Help make a lasting difference in the lives of local families and youth.

  • Enjoy the energy and fun.

    Experience the excitement and joy of kids crossing the finish line.

  • Make a tangible impact.

    Your support ensures the event runs smoothly and safely. Help create memorable experiences that kids will cherish for years to come.
