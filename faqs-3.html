Frequently Asked Questions

  • Who can register for KOS

    Anyone ages 3-17 can register.

  • Do I have to live in the County to register?

    No you don’t have to live in Prince Edward County. While we are a community-focused event and encourage youth in the Quinte region to participate, this event is open to anyone age 3-17.

  • When is the last day I can register?

    May 15th, 2026

  • Is your event suitable for younger kids?

    YES! Our event is very family friendly and we strive to make it as enjoyable as possible for our smallest competitors. The 3-5 year olds are accompanied by a volunteer at each stage of the event.

  • Are floatation devices allowed?

    Lifejackets and floatation devices are allowed and encouraged if the racer is not confident in the water.

  • Can I use training wheels?

    Yes. Training wheels, balance bikes, and tricycles are allowed. However, scooters are not permitted.

  • What if I have a disability?

    We recognize disabilities are unique to each individual. We encourage you to reach out to us at info@countykidsofsteel.ca to see if we can find a way to accommodate your needs in a safe way that allows you to enjoy this experience to its fullest!

  • Is there a financial assistance program?

    YES! It is our mission to put on an event that is affordable for all. We have partnered with The Children’s Foundation to help families with financial barriers sign up for this event. You can apply here: https://www.thechildrensfoundation.ca/playing-for-keeps

  • What do I need to do to prepare my bicycle for the event?

    It’s important that your bike is in safe condition for the event. Check out our Bike Safety Guide to get race ready!

  • What if I've never participated in a triathlon before?

    Welcome! That’s the magic of this event! We encourage everyone (new to tri or experienced) to come out and experience the feel-good, inclusive nature of this event. Everyone deserves an opportunity to step out of their comfort zone, challenge themselves, and feel proud. We will have opportunities leading up to race day to ask us questions, see the race space, and get familiar so that come race day you’ll feel confident and prepared.

  • This will be my first race, I'm scared.

    That makes sense! New experiences can be scary. We will do everything we can to make your first experience a great one! Leading up to race day we will have opportunities for you to tour the venue (PEFAC) and ask any questions. We will also have the route maps posted on our website soon so you can even walk or bike (or both) your age groups route just to get extra familiar! If there are certain things you need to feel more confident in signing up for your first race or triathlon our mailbox is always open! Write us at info@countykidsofsteel.ca with any hesitations, concerns, or questions and we will be happy to do what we can to make this the most positive experience it can be!

  • Do you provide bicycles?

    Participants are required to bring their own bike. For those that need assistance acquiring a bike, Doug’s Bikes is graciously offering the use of some bikes for the day. In addition KOS has some bikes available. While we cannot guarantee that we will have one to offer in the right size, we will have a few bikes available for race day. This is a first come first serve basis. If you are in need of a bike please contact us ahead of race day.

  • Why is it important to know your bib colour?

    The age you are corresponds with the colour of your bib (participant number) the bike and run routes have coloured indicators that let you know when to turn around. Don't worry, there are lots of volunteers who will help you know where to turn too.

  • What is the colour for each age group?

    3-5 Years - Orange
    6-7 Years - Purple
    8-9 Years - Yellow
    10-11 Years - Green
    12-13 Years - Red 
    14-15 Years - Blue
    16-17 Years - Pink
