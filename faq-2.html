FAQ

What is the colour for each age group?

3-5 Years - Orange
6-7 Years - Purple
8-9 Years - Yellow
10-11 Years - Green
12-13 Years - Red 
14-17 Years - Blue

Why is it important to know your bib colour?

The age you are corresponds with the colour of your bib (participant number) the bike and run routes have coloured indicators that let you know when to turn around. Don't worry, there are lots of volunteers who will help you know where to turn too.

Is your event suitable for younger kids?

YES! Our event is very family friendly and we strive to make it as enjoyable as possible for our smallest competitors. The 3-5 year olds are accompanied by a volunteer at each stage of the event.

When is the last day I can register?

Registration Opens February 1st, 2019 and Closes at 12 noon on June 1st, 2019.
