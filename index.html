Misson Statement

County Kids of Steel is a community-driven triathlon for kids aged 3-17 in Prince Edward County, dedicated to inspiring a love of active, healthy living. See About to learn more.

It takes a village to run an event like Kids of Steel. Take action and become a member of our extended KOS family. We’ll be honoured to welcome you on board!
