About County Kids of Steel

County Kids of Steel Triathlon is a community-focused event, open to participants ages 3-17.

Our mission is to foster fitness, perseverance, and fun through sports, while promoting safety, healthy development and local family engagement.

“If you set a goal for yourself and are able to achieve it, you have won your race. Your goal can be to come in first, to improve your performance, or just finish the race it’s up to you.” 

-Dave Scott, six-time Ironman world champion
