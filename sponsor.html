Become a Sponsor of the County Kids of Steel Triathlon

Support youth, healthy living, and community spirit in Prince Edward County by becoming a sponsor.

The County Kids of Steel Triathlon brings together hundreds of families, volunteers, and young athletes for a day of fun, perseverance, and achievement.

Your sponsorship helps make this event possible while showcasing your organization as a champion of youth sport and community wellbeing.

The 2026 race takes place June 7 at the Prince Edward Fitness & Aquatic Centre (PEFAC) in Picton.
